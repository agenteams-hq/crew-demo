## What is included here
Only the original pack PNGs needed for the scene plus the pack `read_me.txt`:
- `Tilesets/` - Grass, Hills, Water, Paths, Fences, etc.
- `Objects/` - trees, bushes, flowers, rocks, plants (Basic Grass Biom things).
- `Characters/` - the basic character spritesheet (not used in the scene; the
  scene uses the project's own all-green band creatures).

The raw pack is **not** redistributed as a downloadable zip; these are the
working source PNGs for the prototype only.
