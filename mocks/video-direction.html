Direction for Fares · before build

The feeling (everything serves this)

Relief. You hand something off, and without fuss it is taken care of. The video should feel like an exhale - calm, premium, unhurried. We barely explain; we let you FEEL "oh, it just did it." Emotion first, features almost invisible.

Opening 10-15s (the hook)

Open on near-empty kraft. A single human line appears as if just spoken - "Sort out the Acme renewal." - then settles. One green pulse: Crew heard you. No UI yet. The hook is the ease of asking, not the product. Then the phone glides up into frame and the app quietly comes alive. ~3s of ask, ~7s of the phone gliding in + the board breathing.

Real screens to feature

Use the REAL app (crew-demo): 1. Crew-working board (hero - "Omar drafting your reply", live dots). 2. One-tap Approve (the green Approve, a thumb tap). 3. The calm Done state. Home only as a glimpse. No settings, no menus - only the beats that carry the feeling.

Motion feel · pace

GLIDESSlow dissolves + float, never hard cuts. The phone drifts up; cards fade in one at a time. Subtle parallax between phone and kraft.
HOLDSLet every beat breathe (2-3s holds). Silence is premium. One thing moves at a time.
WORDSFive short lines across the whole film, max. Fraunces italic, lower third, fades. The product carries it.
SOUNDSoft pad + one gentle tap on Approve + a single settle/exhale on Done. ~28-32s total.
