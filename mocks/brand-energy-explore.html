My read: take Breathable now (more air = instant lift, zero palette risk) and a restrained slice of Colorful (green stays lead, 2-3 soft accents for categories/states). Keep the pixel charm as a seasoning - celebrations, empty states, the opt-in companion surface - NOT on the core working screens, or it cheapens the intelligence-driven calm. The sweet spot is "calm with personality," not "game."

1 · Breathable

More air, bigger margins, fewer elements per screen. No palette change. The biggest perceived lift for the least risk.

2 · Colorful

Green leads + a small warm accent set for categories / states. Controlled, never a rainbow.

3 · Playful / Pixel

A sparing pixel-art motif - crew as little characters, a celebration moment. Personality without cheapening the premium calm.
